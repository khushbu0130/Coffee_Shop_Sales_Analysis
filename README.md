# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data to uncover valuable business insights, customer purchasing patterns, and revenue trends. Through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization techniques, the project helps identify key factors driving sales performance and supports data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-selling products and categories.
- Discover peak sales hours and busiest days.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Track revenue trends over time.
- Generate actionable business insights for growth.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- Evaluated total revenue and sales growth trends.
- Identified best-performing products and categories.
- Analyzed hourly and daily sales patterns.
- Examined customer purchase trends and demand fluctuations.
- Highlighted opportunities to optimize inventory and operations.
